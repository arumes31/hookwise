While maintenance mode is on:

  • Console pages show a maintenance notice. Sign-in, health checks and admin pages such as this one stay available.
  • Webhook requests are rejected with HTTP 503 and are not stored. Whether a sender retries depends on the sender.
  • Stale-endpoint checks keep running, so an endpoint whose timeout is shorter than the maintenance window will raise a stale ticket.
